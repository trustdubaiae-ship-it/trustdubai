Questions

Good to know

Is TrustDubai free for me?+
Yes. Searching, comparing and requesting quotes is completely free. You only pay the business you decide to hire.
What does "verified" mean here?+
A business has had its trade licence and documents checked against official records, and its team verified against Emirates ID. You see the result as three shields on their profile.
How do I get quotes?+
Tell us what you need or open a business profile and send a request with a few details. Verified businesses respond with a quote — and on a matched request, you can receive quotes from more than one trusted option.
Can I trust the reviews?+
Reviews come from real customers, businesses can reply openly, and fakes are detected and removed. Combined with the trust score, you get a far clearer picture than star counts alone.
Do I have to create an account?+
You can browse freely. A quick sign-in is asked when you send a request or leave a review, so businesses can reach you back.
